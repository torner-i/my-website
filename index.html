About Me

Hi, I'm Ignacio, I'm from Barcelona, and I'm a Computer Science student at Northeastern University, minoring in Mathematics, with a strong pull toward AI engineering and cybersecurity. I enjoy tackling problems where automation, data, and security intersect, and I'm always open to new projects and roles in that space.

I've worked as an AI Engineer Intern at Nertor S.A. in Barcelona, building automation tools with Python and the Amazon SP-API, including dynamic pricing systems, sales reporting pipelines, and real-time inventory dashboards. I hold the Google Cybersecurity Professional Certificate and I've applied that background in projects like a secure authentication platform with brute-force protection and audit logging.

Outside of class, I like to stay busy with my hobbies. I enjoy reading, climbing, and exploring Boston with my friends. My favorite hobby, though, is playing soccer. I've been playing since I was 6, and I usually play as a defender.

My favorite team is FC Barcelona. Growing up in Barcelona, I've been surrounded by Barça fans for as long as I can remember. My dad and I used to watch matches together every weekend, and he taught me the players' names and chants. Supporting Barça has always been a big part of my life.

A few years ago I got to visit Camp Nou, Barça's stadium in Barcelona. It was honestly one of the best days of my life. To learn more about how Barcelona became one of the biggest clubs in the world, check out the Club History page.
